Built around your day

Every room has a purpose. Every room is Redroot.

Most apps give you one tool and ask you to use ten others alongside it. Redroot gives you one system — many specialized surfaces, one shared design language, one source of truth.

📅

Calendar & Timeline

Navigate your month, week, and day. Switch to an hourly timeline with a live "now" line — always showing you exactly where you are in the day.

✓

Tasks & Lists

Commit things that need a date. Organize things that don't. Overdue, Today, Upcoming, and Undefined — your task stack is always correctly sorted.

↻

Habits

Build your rhythm. Track daily habits with completion history, streaks, and a day-by-day view that keeps your routines visible alongside everything else.

◑

State tracking

Optional modules for mental state, sleep, body, and intake. Know how you're running — mood, energy, rest, and movement — as part of your daily record.

✦

Notes & Voice

Capture fast with text or voice. Notes live alongside your calendar, not buried in a separate app you have to remember to open.

◎

AI that knows your day

An AI assistant with full context — your tasks, habits, notes, and calendar — ready to think through your day with you, organized by project workspace.

Design principle

Live, not static

Redroot tracks where you are in the day right now — not just what you scheduled last Tuesday. The NOW section, the live timeline, and the overdue stack update as your day moves.

Architecture

Many rooms, one system

Habits are not tasks. Tasks are not lists. Lists are not notes. Each surface has a single verb and stays out of the others' way. Controlled depth, not cluttered simplicity.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

Redroot is powered by iCloud. Your data lives in your iCloud account, syncs across your devices, and is never sold or shared. No accounts, no logins beyond Apple ID.
