Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026  ·  Redroot, developed by RHS

The short version: Redroot stores your data in your own iCloud account. We do not collect it, sell it, or have access to it. The only time data leaves your device is when you explicitly use the AI assistant or voice transcription features — and only the content you submit in that moment is sent.

What Redroot stores and where

All data you create in Redroot — events, tasks, lists, habits, goals, notes, voice recordings, medications, finance records, sleep data, and health check-ins — is stored exclusively in your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework.

This means:

  • Your data lives in your iCloud account, not on our servers
  • It syncs across your own devices automatically via Apple's infrastructure
  • We do not have a backend server that stores, processes, or has access to your data
  • Deleting the app or your iCloud data removes it entirely

Data we do not collect

Redroot does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to our servers
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Browsing or behavioral data

We have no user accounts. Redroot uses your Apple ID solely for iCloud authentication, which is handled entirely by Apple.

AI assistant

Redroot includes an optional AI chat feature. When you send a message to the AI assistant, that message — along with limited context from your app data that you choose to share — is transmitted to a third-party AI provider to generate a response.

Regarding this feature:

  • Data is only sent when you actively use the chat feature
  • Conversations are not stored on our servers
  • The AI provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle submitted content
  • You can choose not to use this feature — it has no effect on the rest of the app

Voice transcription

The voice notes feature optionally uses AssemblyAI to transcribe audio recordings into text. If you configure and use this feature:

  • Your audio recording is transmitted to AssemblyAI's servers for transcription
  • AssemblyAI's privacy policy governs how they handle submitted audio
  • Transcription is optional — voice recordings can be stored as audio only without transcription
  • You supply your own AssemblyAI API key in settings; Redroot does not collect or transmit it

GitHub integration

Redroot includes an optional GitHub integration for use within project workspaces. If you configure this feature, requests are made directly from your device to GitHub's API using credentials you provide. GitHub's privacy policy governs how they handle those requests. Your GitHub credentials are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

iCloud and Apple

Because Redroot uses iCloud for all data storage and sync, Apple's iCloud and privacy policies apply to the underlying storage and sync infrastructure. You can review Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Redroot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
