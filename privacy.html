Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026  ·  Redroot, developed by RHS

The short version: Redroot stores everything in your own iCloud account. We have no backend server and collect no analytics. The only time data leaves your device is when you explicitly use an optional feature that requires it.

Data storage

All data you create in Redroot — events, tasks, lists, habits, goals, notes, voice recordings, medications, finance records, sleep logs, mental state check-ins, period tracking, intake logs, and project files — is stored exclusively in your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework.

  • Your data lives in your iCloud account, not on our servers
  • It syncs across your own devices automatically via Apple's infrastructure
  • We have no backend server that stores, processes, or has access to your data
  • Deleting the app or your iCloud data removes it entirely

Data we do not collect

Redroot does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to our servers
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data

We have no user accounts. Your Apple ID is used solely for iCloud authentication, which is handled entirely by Apple.

Health & sensor data

Some Redroot modules access health and sensor data on your device:

  • HealthKit — used to read and write sleep data when the Sleep module is enabled
  • CoreMotion — accelerometer data is used locally for automatic sleep detection; it is never transmitted
  • EventKit — used to read your Apple Calendar events when external calendar sync is enabled
  • Photos — used when you attach images to notes or events

All sensor and health data stays on your device or in your iCloud account. None of it is transmitted to us.

Watch app

The Redroot Watch app syncs intake, medication, and sleep logs with your iPhone via iCloud Key-Value storage. No data is transmitted to any server — sync happens through Apple's infrastructure only.

AI assistant

Redroot's AI chat feature supports multiple configurations:

  • On-device (default) — runs a local model entirely on your device; no data leaves
  • External provider — if you configure an OpenAI-compatible or Ollama endpoint, messages and any context you share are sent to that provider
  • Conversations are not stored on our servers regardless of provider
  • You can use the app fully without ever enabling the AI feature

When using an external provider, that provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle submitted content.

Voice transcription

Voice notes can optionally be transcribed using AssemblyAI. If you configure and use this feature:

  • Your audio is transmitted to AssemblyAI's servers for transcription
  • AssemblyAI's privacy policy governs how they handle submitted audio
  • Transcription is optional — recordings can be kept as audio only
  • You supply your own AssemblyAI API key; Redroot does not collect or transmit it

GitHub integration

The optional GitHub integration makes requests directly from your device to GitHub's API using a token you provide. Your token is stored locally and is not transmitted to us. GitHub's privacy policy governs those requests.

iCloud and Apple

Because Redroot uses iCloud for all data storage and sync, Apple's privacy policies apply to the underlying infrastructure. You can review Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Redroot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
